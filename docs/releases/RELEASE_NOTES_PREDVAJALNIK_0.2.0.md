# Safeer Predvajalnik 0.2.0 (Android)

Visible controls instead of hidden gestures, plus two fixes from 0.1.0.

**New**
- Audio track selection (button appears when a video has more than one audio track, e.g. languages or commentary).
- Back 10 s / forward 10 s buttons and a draggable progress bar (seek by dragging, applied on release).
- Picture in picture: a button in the player, automatic on Home while a video plays (Android 12+; on 8–11 via the Home key), play/pause button inside the small window.

**Fixed**
- Files opened from a file manager showed a number instead of the file name.
- Back from Safeer Media led to the Safeer OS home screen, which is not part of this app; Back now leaves the app.

Slovensko: **Vidni gumbi namesto skritih kretenj** in dva popravka iz 0.1.0. **Novo:** izbira zvočne sledi (gumb se pokaže, ko ima video več sledi – jeziki, komentar); gumba nazaj/naprej 10 s in drsnik po posnetku (premik ob spustu); slika v sliki (gumb v predvajalniku, samodejno ob tipki Domov med videom na Androidu 12+, na 8–11 prek tipke Domov; gumb predvajaj/premor v malem oknu). **Popravljeno:** datoteka iz upravitelja datotek je kazala številko namesto imena; Nazaj iz Safeer Media je vodil na domači zaslon Safeer OS, ki ni del te aplikacije – zdaj Nazaj zapusti aplikacijo.

Preverjeno na telefonu z Androidom 14 (zvočne sledi, gumbi, drsnik, slika v sliki, »Odpri z«, Nazaj). Namestitev čez 0.1.0 (isti podpis).
