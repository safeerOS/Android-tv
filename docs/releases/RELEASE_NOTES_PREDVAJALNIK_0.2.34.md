# Safeer Predvajalnik 0.2.34

- »Nadaljuj gledanje« in »Nazadnje predvajano« ne kažeta več naslovov dodatka, ki ga nimaš več.
- Ko film iz torrenta prepustiš drugi napravi v Safeer Linku, ji predvajalnik pove naslov in plakat (za polico »Na tvojih napravah« v Safeer OS); naslov iz zasebnega dodatka gre brez naslova in plakata.

English: “Continue watching” and “Recently played” no longer show titles of an add-on you no longer have. When a film from a torrent is handed to another device in Safeer Link, the player tells it the title and poster (for the “On your devices” shelf in Safeer OS); a title from a private add-on is sent without them.
