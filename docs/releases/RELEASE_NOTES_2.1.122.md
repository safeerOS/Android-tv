# Safeer Browser for Android TV 2.1.122 · Safeer OS 0.4.5 · Safeer OS Tablet 0.3.5

**New: Safeer Media – music, radio and video in one place, without ads.**

- **Home screen of Media:** four categories (Music, Radio, Video, Sources), *Now playing* with a large cover and controls (shuffle, previous, play, next, repeat, favourite), quick actions (dim screen, playback speed, sleep timer, stop), *Recent* and *Your sources*. Everything fits on the first screen of the TV – no half-cut rows.
- **One search for everything:** music, artists, radio stations, PeerTube videos and your sources. Results must contain all the words you typed. If nothing fits, the first card opens the web search directly on the *Videos* tab.
- **Player:** press Down for search, favourite, save queue as a playlist, shuffle/repeat and suggestions. Back returns to Safeer Media, not to the Safeer OS home screen.
- **Favourites and playlists:** a whole playlist can be added to favourites.
- **Web sources** (e.g. rtvslo.si) open in the desktop layout, with a single focus frame and faster switching; their sound keeps playing in the background, and Back returns to Media.
- Free sources: Jamendo (music), internet radio, PeerTube (video).

Slovensko: **Novo – Safeer Media: glasba, radio in video na enem mestu, brez oglasov.** Nadzorna plošča s štirimi kategorijami, *Zdaj se predvaja* z veliko naslovnico in upravljanjem, hitra dejanja (zatemnitev, hitrost, časovnik za spanje, ustavi), *Nedavno* in *Tvoji viri* – vse na prvem zaslonu televizorja. Eno iskanje po glasbi, izvajalcih, postajah, videih PeerTube in tvojih virih; če ni zadetka, prva kartica odpre spletno iskanje pod *Videoposnetki*. V predvajalniku tipka dol odpre iskanje, priljubljene, shranjevanje vrste in predloge; nazaj vrne v Safeer Media. Seznam predvajanja lahko dodaš med priljubljene. Spletni viri (npr. rtvslo.si) se odprejo v namizni postavitvi z enim samim okvirjem, zvok teče v ozadju, nazaj vrne v Medije.
