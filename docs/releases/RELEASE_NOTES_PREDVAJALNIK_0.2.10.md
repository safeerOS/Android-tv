# Safeer Predvajalnik 0.2.10

- **Pretvori za televizor:** ko je Predvajalnik v Safeer Linku edina Safeer aplikacija na napravi, lahko s strojnim kodirnikom pretvori video računalnika v H.264 do 1080p (mapa *Prenosi › Safeer Shramba*). Če je na napravi Safeer OS, to opravi Safeer OS.

English: Convert for TV – when the player is the only Safeer app on the device, it can convert a computer's video to H.264 up to 1080p with the hardware encoder (*Downloads › Safeer Shramba*). If Safeer OS is on the device, Safeer OS does it.
