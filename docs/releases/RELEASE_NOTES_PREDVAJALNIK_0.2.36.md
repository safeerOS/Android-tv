# Safeer Predvajalnik 0.2.36

- **Film se začne z virom, ki dela.** Predvajalnik rok časovno omejene povezave prebere iz same povezave: potekle ne poskusi, med enakovrednima viroma ima prednost tisti brez roka. Mrtev vir preskoči takoj in brez obvestila; če vir odpove sredi filma, naslednji nadaljuje pri istem mestu.
- **»Ni na voljo« samo, ko res ni.** Kadar dodatek ne odgovori, Predvajalnik to pove in naslov ostane na zaslonu; v ozadju pošilja dodatkom precej manj poizvedb.

English: a film starts with a source that works – an expired link is never tried, a permanent link is preferred between two equal sources, a dead source is skipped at once and silently, and a switch in the middle of a film continues from the same position. When an add-on does not respond the player says so and the title stays on screen; far fewer background requests are sent to add-ons.
