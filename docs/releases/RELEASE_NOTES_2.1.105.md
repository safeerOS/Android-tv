# Safeer Browser for Android TV 2.1.105 · Safeer OS 0.2.5

**Playback survives a hiccup in the network, Safeer OS speaks your language, and signing in works again.**

- **A short network outage no longer ends the film.** When the connection drops for a moment, playback now retries on its own up to three times, and the OK key starts it again instead of doing nothing.
- **Video playback follows one general rule** instead of adjustments written for individual websites, so it behaves the same everywhere.
- **Sign-in with Google, Facebook and X works again**, and pop-up windows no longer have side effects on the pages that open them.
- **Safeer OS speaks your language.** The settings, the screen view and the session menu now use the chosen language, and Safeer OS has its own language setting (English, Slovenian, German, Spanish, French, Italian, or follow the television).
- **A page sent from another device** is announced in your language too; until now that one message stayed Slovenian.
- **Safeer OS: a Continue row on the home screen** takes you back to what you were watching or doing, and the app carries its legal notices (responsibility, and that it is built with the help of artificial intelligence).
- An honest hint for the gamepad: the app says what the remote can do instead of promising more.
- The project is public: an English README, a contributing guide and a code of conduct.
