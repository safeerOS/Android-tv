# Safeer Predvajalnik 0.2.50

- **Safeer Link: ukazi med napravami so podpisani s ključem naprave in šifrirani od naprave do naprave.** Ko imata obe napravi to izdajo (Predvajalnik 0.2.50 ali Safeer OS 0.5.64 na napravi z Androidom, Safeer Control 2.1.62 na računalniku), o dostopu odloča ključ naprave, ne oznaka, ki jo v sporočilo vpiše središče; središče ukazov in odgovorov nanje ne vidi. Ko naprava ključ enkrat dokaže, ta naprava v njenem imenu ne sprejme več nezaščitenega ukaza. Starejše naprave delajo kot prej, dokler jih ne posodobiš – in so zaščitene le toliko kot prej.
- Vsebuje tudi spremembo iz Safeer OS 0.5.63: dotik gledalca deljenega zaslona velja samo z znakom deljenja in samo, če je napravi, s katero deliš zaslon, odprto »Zaslon in upravljanje«.

Ista koda Safeer Linka kot v Safeer OS 0.5.64; kaj je bilo izmerjeno v živo in kaj ta izdaja ne zapira, je v opombah izdaje Safeer OS 0.5.64 (v2.1.188). Meritve v živo so bile narejene s Safeer OS 0.5.64 na telefonu; končna gradnja Predvajalnika ni bila posebej preizkušena v živo.

English: Safeer Link: commands between devices are signed with the device key and encrypted from device to device. Once both devices run this release (Player 0.2.50 or Safeer OS 0.5.64 on an Android device, Safeer Control 2.1.62 on a computer), access is decided by the device key, not by the id the hub writes into a message, and the hub sees neither the commands nor their answers. Once a device has proven its key, this device no longer accepts an unprotected command in its name. Older devices keep working as before until updated and are only as protected as before. Also contains the change from Safeer OS 0.5.63: a touch from the viewer of a shared screen counts only with the share mark and only if the device you share the screen with has "Screen and control" open. Same Safeer Link code as Safeer OS 0.5.64; what was measured live and what this release does not close is listed in the release notes of Safeer OS 0.5.64 (v2.1.188). The live measurements were made with Safeer OS 0.5.64 on a phone; the final build of the Player was not separately tested live.
