# Safeer OS 0.5.11 (TV, tablica, telefon) · Safeer Browser za Android TV 2.1.135

- **Kretnje na zaslonu predvajanja (telefon, tablica):** poteg gor/dol na levi strani slike spremeni svetlost, na desni glasnost; dvojni dotik levo/desno pri videu previje 10 s. Kretnje delujejo samo na sliki – vsi gumbi ostanejo, kjer so.
- **Video pokonci:** video je zgoraj, gumbi in vrsta *Še za ogled* pod njim – nič več ne prekriva slike.

English: Touch devices get VLC-style gestures on the playback screen (swipe left side for brightness, right side for volume, double-tap to skip 10 s in videos – only on the picture, all buttons stay) and portrait video now sits at the top with the controls below it. No browser changes in 2.1.135.
