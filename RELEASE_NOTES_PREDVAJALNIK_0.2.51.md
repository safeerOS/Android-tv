# Safeer Predvajalnik 0.2.51

- **Safeer Link: ukazi med napravami so podpisani s ključem naprave in šifrirani od naprave do naprave.** Ko imata obe napravi to izdajo (Predvajalnik 0.2.51 ali Safeer OS 0.5.65 na napravi z Androidom, Safeer Control 2.1.63 na računalniku), o dostopu odloča ključ naprave, ne oznaka, ki jo v sporočilo vpiše središče; središče ukazov in odgovorov nanje ne vidi. Ko naprava ključ enkrat dokaže, ta naprava v njenem imenu ne sprejme več nezaščitenega ukaza. Starejše naprave delajo kot prej, dokler jih ne posodobiš – in so zaščitene le toliko kot prej.
- Različica 0.2.50 je bila pripravljena, a **ni bila objavljena**: drugi neodvisni pregled je pred objavo našel napake v zaščiti (med drugim: oznaka naprave s prelomom vrstice je napravi, ki gosti središče, omogočila, da se je ukaz ene naprave izvedel na drugi). Ta izdaja jih odpravlja in je šla pred objavo skozi še štiri preglede (skupaj šest; popravki po šestem niso bili pregledani še sedmič); opis je v opombah izdaje Safeer OS 0.5.65 (v2.1.189).
- Vsebuje tudi spremembo iz Safeer OS 0.5.63: dotik gledalca deljenega zaslona velja samo z znakom deljenja in samo, če je napravi, s katero deliš zaslon, odprto »Zaslon in upravljanje«.

Ista koda Safeer Linka kot v Safeer OS 0.5.65; kaj je bilo izmerjeno v živo in kaj ta izdaja ne zapira, je v opombah izdaje Safeer OS 0.5.65 (v2.1.189). Meritve v živo so bile narejene s Safeer OS 0.5.65 na dveh telefonih; končna gradnja Predvajalnika je bila nanju nameščena, ukazi Predvajalniku pa v živo niso bili posebej izmerjeni.

English: Safeer Link: commands between devices are signed with the device key and encrypted from device to device. Once both devices run this release (Player 0.2.51 or Safeer OS 0.5.65 on an Android device, Safeer Control 2.1.63 on a computer), access is decided by the device key, not by the id the hub writes into a message, and the hub sees neither the commands nor their answers. Once a device has proven its key, this device no longer accepts an unprotected command in its name. Older devices keep working as before until updated and are only as protected as before. Version 0.2.50 was prepared but **not published**: a second independent review found defects in the protection before publication (among them: a device id containing a line break let a device hosting the hub have one device's command run on another device). This release fixes them and went through four more reviews before publication (six in total; the fixes made after the sixth were not reviewed a seventh time); see the release notes of Safeer OS 0.5.65 (v2.1.189). Also contains the change from Safeer OS 0.5.63: a touch from the viewer of a shared screen counts only with the share mark and only if the device you share the screen with has "Screen and control" open. Same Safeer Link code as Safeer OS 0.5.65; what was measured live and what this release does not close is listed in the release notes of Safeer OS 0.5.65 (v2.1.189). The live measurements were made with Safeer OS 0.5.65 on two phones; the final build of the Player was installed on them, but commands to the Player were not separately measured live.
