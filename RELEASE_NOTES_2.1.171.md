# Safeer 2.1.171 · Safeer OS 0.5.47

Internet prek Safeer Linka (Safeer OS Mobile) – telefon posodi svojo internetno povezavo računalniku v Safeer Linku, brez dostopne točke:

- **Veliki prenosi držijo.** Do 0.5.46 je deljenje delovalo samo za majhne zahteve: prenos, hitrejši od Linka, je napolnil vrsto središča in telefon je izgubil povezavo z Linkom (izmerjeno: prenos 20 MB je obstal pri 18 MB). Novi protokol omeji, kar je na poti, oštevilči vsak kos – izgubljen kos povezavo konča, namesto da bi jo pokvaril – in si sam opomore, ko se računalnik ali aplikacija na telefonu zažene znova.
- **Dovoljenje za vsako napravo posebej.** Ko računalnik prvič prosi za internet, telefon vpraša: z obvestilom z gumboma Dovoli in Zavrni in z oknom, ko je Safeer OS na zaslonu ali ga odpreš. Okna iz ozadja Android ne dovoli odpreti, obvestila pa so lahko izklopljena – zato se vprašanje pokaže tudi ob odprtju aplikacije. Odločitev kadarkoli spremeniš v Nastavitve › Internet prek Safeer Linka.
- **Mobilni podatki so svoje stikalo**, gostovanje še eno. Nastaviš lahko mesečno omejitev; nastavitve pokažejo porabo danes in ta mesec.
- **Telefon ostane na Wi-Fi-ju.** Mobilno omrežje vklopi samo za povezave, ki jih posreduje (izmerjeno: v manj kot pol sekunde, tudi z ugasnjenim zaslonom). Naslovov v svojem domačem omrežju in vrat za pošiljanje pošte računalniku ne odpira.
- Nastavitve so prevedene v vseh šest jezikov (prej samo slovensko).

Na računalniku je za to potreben Safeer Control 2.1.47 (Linux): Safeer OS › Omrežne povezave › Internet prek telefona ali `safeerctl internet`. Izmerjeno po mobilnem omrežju zmogljivega telefona z ugasnjenim zaslonom: 11 MB/s za eno povezavo, 17 MB/s za osem hkrati, vsaka datoteka enaka bajt za bajtom; skozi telefon srednjega razreda 5,6 in 7 MB/s.

Medijski center – seznami filmov in serij (vse aplikacije):

- **Seznam se ne polni več od začetka.** Safeer pokaže film šele, ko pri dodatkih preveri, da se ga res da predvajati, dodatek pa dovoli le nekaj vprašanj na minuto – nov seznam se zato polni po eno kartico na osem sekund. To ostaja. Odpravljeno je vse, zaradi česar se je isto preverjalo znova in znova: vsaka sprememba dodatkov (tudi dodatka s podnapisi, in ker se viri usklajujejo, na vseh napravah hkrati) je pozabila vse odgovore; »ni na voljo« je poteklo vsak dan in seznam je spet čakal; sprememba pomočnika za torrente ali prostega prostora je pomenila prazen seznam. Zdaj odgovor ostane, velja za vsak način naprave in se osveži v ozadju – seznam čaka samo še na naslove, ki jih še nihče ni preveril. Izmerjeno na telefonu: seznam Filmi, ki se je po taki spremembi polnil od začetka (3 kartice po 1,5 s, 12 po 90 s), je zdaj poln v 2–3 s.
- **Naprave si odgovore delijo.** Kar je preveril telefon, tablici in televizorju v Safeer Linku ni treba preverjati še enkrat – eno vprašanje na dom, ne na napravo. Izmerjeno: telefon, ki seznama sam ni nikoli preveril, ga je od drugega dobil ob odprtju Medijskega centra in pokazal polnega v 3 s. Velja za naprave z istimi dodatki; delijo se samo javne oznake filmov in serij, vsebina zasebnih dodatkov ostane na napravi.
- **Manj vprašanj dodatku.** Dodatek šteje vprašanja po domačem naslovu, ne po napravi – več naprav hkrati ga hitro pripelje do omejitve, in takrat ne da niti toka za film. Preverjanje se zato ustavi, ko zapustiš zaslon (prej je v ozadju teklo naprej), odgovori veljajo dlje, in ko dodatek omeji eno napravo, počakajo vse naprave v Safeer Linku.

Safeer Link (vse aplikacije):

- **Sporočila grejo brez zakasnitve.** Kratko sporočilo je lahko čakalo na potrditev prejšnjega (Naglov algoritem), kar je pri hitri izmenjavi pomenilo postanke po nekaj deset milisekund. Zdaj gre vsako sporočilo takoj.
- **Središče hitreje obdela velika sporočila** (branje okvirjev in JSON brez odvečnega kopiranja); skupaj s popravkom na računalniku je prepustnost skozi telefon srednjega razreda zrasla z 2,3 na 5,6 MB/s.
- Ko se povezava s sosednjim središčem konča, dnevnik pove zakaj.

Še ne gre: UDP (in z njim QUIC/HTTP3 – brskalniki preklopijo na TCP); računalnik z Windows, televizor ali tablica kot prejemnik interneta; povezava, ki izgubi kos, se konča in program poskusi znova.

English: **Internet over Safeer Link (Safeer OS Mobile)** – the phone lends its internet connection to a computer in Safeer Link, without a hotspot. **Large transfers hold:** up to 0.5.46 sharing worked only for small requests – a download faster than Link could carry filled the hub's queue and the phone lost its Link connection (measured: a 20 MB download stalled at 18 MB). The new protocol bounds what is on its way, numbers every chunk – a lost one ends the connection instead of corrupting it – and recovers by itself when the computer or the phone app restarts. **Permission per device:** the first time a computer asks, the phone asks you – with a notification (Allow / Deny) and with a dialog when Safeer OS is on screen or when you open it. Android does not let a background app open a window and notifications may be switched off, so the question also appears when the app is opened. The decision can be changed at any time in Settings › Internet over Safeer Link. **Mobile data is its own switch**, roaming another; a monthly limit can be set and the settings show today's and this month's use. **The phone stays on Wi-Fi** and brings the mobile network up only for the connections it forwards (measured: in under half a second, also with the screen off); it does not open addresses in its own home network or mail-sending ports for the computer. The settings are now translated into all six languages. On the computer this needs Safeer Control 2.1.47 (Linux): Safeer OS › Network connections › Internet through the phone, or `safeerctl internet`. Measured over the mobile network of a current flagship phone with the screen off: 11 MB/s for one connection, 17 MB/s for eight in parallel, every file identical byte for byte; through a mid-range phone 5.6 and 7 MB/s. **Media centre – film and series lists (all apps):** a list no longer fills from scratch. Safeer shows a film only after the add-ons have confirmed that it can be played, and an add-on allows only a few questions a minute – a new list therefore fills one card every eight seconds. That stays. What is gone is everything that made Safeer ask the same thing again and again: every change of add-ons (even a subtitles add-on – and since sources sync, on all devices at once) forgot all the answers; “not available” expired every day and the list waited again; a change of the torrent helper or of free space meant an empty list. An answer now stays, holds for every mode of the device and is refreshed in the background – a list waits only for titles nobody has checked yet. Measured on a phone: the Films list, which after such a change filled from scratch (3 cards after 1.5 s, 12 after 90 s), is now full in 2–3 s. Devices share the answers: what the phone has checked, the tablet and the television in Safeer Link do not check again – one question per home, not per device (measured: a phone that had never checked the list got it from another one when the media centre opened and showed it full in 3 s; devices with the same add-ons only; only public film and series ids are shared, what private add-ons contain stays on the device). And fewer questions reach the add-on: it counts them per home address, not per device – several devices at once quickly run into its limit, and then it does not even return a stream for a film. Checking therefore stops when you leave the screen (it used to continue in the background), answers stay valid longer, and when the add-on limits one device, all devices in Safeer Link wait. **Safeer Link (all apps):** messages go out without delay – a short message could wait for the previous one to be acknowledged (Nagle's algorithm), which meant pauses of some tens of milliseconds in a quick exchange; the hub handles large messages faster (frames and JSON without needless copying) – together with the fix on the computer, throughput through a mid-range phone rose from 2.3 to 5.6 MB/s; and when the connection to a neighbouring hub ends, the log says why. Not yet: UDP (and with it QUIC/HTTP3 – browsers fall back to TCP); a Windows computer, a television or a tablet as the receiving side; a connection that loses a chunk ends and the program retries.
