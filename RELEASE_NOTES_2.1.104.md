# Safeer Browser for Android TV 2.1.104 · Safeer OS 0.2.4

**Your computer on the television: its screen, its files and its programs — and a remote that really controls them.**

- **Your computer's screen on the TV.** Safeer OS asks the computer for a session and shows its desktop with sound, over one encrypted connection with a pinned certificate and a one-time token. The remote moves the mouse pointer (it accelerates while you hold a direction), OK clicks, a long OK is a right click; Menu switches to plain keys for programs that expect them, and there is an on-screen keyboard with a session menu (keyboard, keys, save, shortcuts, end session).
- **A held key is now really held.** Holding a direction used to arrive at the computer as a burst of separate taps, so games stuttered and long scrolling did not work. The television now sends the press and the release, and the computer holds the key exactly as a finger would. If the connection drops mid-hold, the computer releases the key by itself.
- **Your computer's programs, sorted.** The programs list is grouped the way your desktop menu groups them — Games, Office, Web, Media, Programming, Learning, Tools — with the number in each group, and a search box that narrows the list as you type. Starting a program now switches straight to the computer's screen, so you see what you opened instead of staying on a list.
- **A gamepad for the whole system.** A controller connected to the television drives all of Safeer OS: the stick and the D-pad move the selection, A opens, B goes back, X edits, Y is the home screen, the shoulders jump between rows and pages, Start opens settings. When the computer supports it, the controller goes straight through to the game on the computer as a real gamepad.
- **Files the TV cannot open are opened by the computer.** No warning, no dead end: text files are shown on the television itself, everything else (documents, spreadsheets, archives) opens on the computer, and when the computer is sharing its screen you see it here immediately.
- **Backgrounds of your own.** The background is now chosen separately from the theme — abstract waves, mountains, forest, glow, sea, or your own photograph from the computer — with a dimming slider so the text stays readable.
- **Apps look like apps.** Sharper icons fetched at the size the television actually shows, white plates removed, two-word titles on two lines, and the bottom row is no longer cut off.
- **Start with the television, and see what it can do.** Safeer OS can start when you switch the TV on, and Performance now reports the processor, memory, disk and the graphics card of the computer it is connected to.
- **Lighter and less demanding.** Safeer OS no longer asks for the microphone or for drawing over other apps, and it no longer requests a large heap; memory use at start dropped from about 110 MB to under 60 MB.
- **Checksums that tell the truth.** SHA256SUMS is written during every build, so it can no longer name an older package and suggest yours was tampered with.

Slovensko: **Zaslon računalnika na televizorju** s sliko in zvokom po eni šifrirani povezavi; daljinec premika miškin kazalec (ob držanju se pospešuje), OK klikne, dolg OK je desni klik, Meni preklopi na tipke, na voljo pa sta zaslonska tipkovnica in meni seje. **Držana tipka je zdaj res držana** — prej je vsako držanje na računalnik prišlo kot vrsta kratkih pritiskov, zato igre niso bile igrljive; ob prekinjeni povezavi računalnik tipko sam spusti. **Programi računalnika so razvrščeni v skupine** (Igre, Pisarna, Splet, Predstavnost, Programiranje, Učenje, Orodja) s številom v vsaki in iskanjem po imenu, zagon programa pa takoj preklopi na zaslon računalnika. **Igralni plošček upravlja ves Safeer OS**, in kadar računalnik to zna, gre naravnost v igro na računalniku kot pravi plošček. **Datoteke, ki jih televizor ne zna odpreti, odpre računalnik** brez opozorila; besedilo prebereš kar tu. **Ozadje izbereš ločeno od teme** (valovi, gore, gozd, sij, morje ali lastna fotografija) z drsnikom zatemnitve. **Aplikacije izgledajo kot aplikacije**: ostrejše ikone, brez belih podlag, dve besedi v dveh vrsticah, spodnja vrstica ni odrezana. **Safeer OS se lahko zažene ob vklopu televizorja**, Zmogljivost pa pove tudi grafično kartico računalnika. **Manj zahteven**: brez mikrofona in risanja čez druge aplikacije, poraba ob zagonu s ~110 MB na manj kot 60 MB. **Kontrolne vsote** nastanejo ob vsaki gradnji in ne kažejo več na starejši paket.
