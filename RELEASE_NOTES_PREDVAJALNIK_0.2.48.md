# Safeer Predvajalnik 0.2.48

Brskanje po datotekah z drugih naprav (zavihek Brskaj) – naprava, ki ni doma:

- **Datoteke naprave, ki je na mobilnih podatkih.** Naprava brez domačega omrežja (Safeer OS 0.5.58) zdaj pove, da je pot do njenih datotek samo prek njenega središča (Global Link). Predvajalnik gre tja takoj – brez poskusa neposredne povezave, ki ne more uspeti.

Ista koda branja datotek kot v Safeer OS 0.5.58. Samodejni preizkus: naslov brez domačega omrežja pomeni pot samo prek središča. V Predvajalniku samem ta izdaja ni bila preizkušena v živo.

English: Browsing files from your other devices (Browse tab) – a device that is away from home: a device without a home network (Safeer OS 0.5.58) now says that the only way to its files is through its hub (Global Link), and the player goes there at once, without a direct attempt that cannot succeed. Same file-reading code as Safeer OS 0.5.58; covered by an automated test, not tested live in the player itself.
