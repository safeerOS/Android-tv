# Safeer Predvajalnik 0.2.49

- **Nov video se ne začne več v premoru.** Kadar si nov video zagnal, medtem ko je bil odprt zaslon predvajanja prejšnjega, se je novi začel v premoru; na televizorju je po nadaljevanju ostala zamrznjena slika.
- **Zvočna sled z več kanali.** Kadar ima datoteka v istem jeziku več zvočnih sledi, predvajalnik izbere tisto, ki da na izhodu naprave več kanalov (na primer Dolby Digital Plus 5.1 namesto privzete stereo sledi AAC); sledi s komentarjem ne izbere sam. Ročna izbira ima prednost.
- **Oznaka zvoka pod naslovom** pove, kaj gre iz zvočnikov, kadar to ni navaden stereo: na primer »Dolby Atmos« ali »Dolby Digital Plus 5.1«, kadar je tok predan zvočniku, in na televizorju »5.1 → stereo«, kadar prostorski zvok do zvočnika ne pride. Pri formatu, ki ga priključeni zvočnik ne navaja, piše samo ime formata.
- **Safeer Link:** kaj od vsebin druge naprave je na voljo, odloča ta naprava sama (Safeer OS 0.5.62: Safeer Link → naprava → »Dostop te naprave«; Safeer Control 2.1.61 na računalniku).

Ista koda predvajanja kot v Safeer OS 0.5.62. Popravki so bili izmerjeni in preverjeni s preizkusnimi gradnjami te kode na telefonu in televizorju; končna gradnja Predvajalnika ni bila posebej preizkušena v živo.

English: A new video no longer starts paused when it is started while the playback screen of the previous one is open (on a TV the picture stayed frozen after resuming). When a file has several audio tracks in the same language, the player picks the one that gives more channels on the device's output (for example Dolby Digital Plus 5.1 instead of the default AAC stereo track) and does not pick a commentary track by itself; a manual choice wins. A label under the title says what comes out of the speakers when it is not plain stereo – "Dolby Atmos" or "Dolby Digital Plus 5.1" when the stream is passed to the speaker, and on a TV "5.1 → stereo" when surround sound does not reach the speaker; for a format the connected speaker does not list, only the format name is shown. Safeer Link: what of another device's content is available is decided by that device itself (Safeer OS 0.5.62, Safeer Control 2.1.61). Same playback code as Safeer OS 0.5.62; the fixes were measured and verified with test builds of this code on a phone and a TV, the final build of the player was not separately tested live.
