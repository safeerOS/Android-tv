# Safeer Browser for Android TV 2.1.124 · Safeer OS 0.4.7 · Safeer OS Tablet 0.3.7

**The remote controls a media player on the computer like a media player.**

- When a program on the computer's separate TV screen is a media player (VLC, Celluloid, Totem, Rhythmbox …), the remote no longer moves a pointer: OK plays and pauses, left and right skip 10 seconds (hold for 30), up and down show the playback bar.
- The playback bar is drawn by the TV itself: title, artist, time and progress, the same for every player. It hides after a few seconds.
- The menu of the TV screen can switch between player and pointer at any time; the choice is remembered per program.
- The session survives a short drop of the connection: the TV reconnects for up to 30 seconds instead of closing the screen.
- Needs Safeer Control 2.1.1 or later on the computer.

**Tablet held upright.** Settings, Appearance and the TV apps now turn with the tablet, and the Apps grid shows four columns instead of six squeezed ones.

Slovensko: **Daljinec upravlja predvajalnik na računalniku kot predvajalnik.** Ko na ločenem zaslonu teče predvajalnik (VLC, Celluloid, Totem, Rhythmbox …), OK predvaja in ustavi, levo in desno previjeta za 10 s (zadržano 30 s), gor in dol pokažeta pas za predvajanje z naslovom, časom in napredkom – enak v vseh predvajalnikih. V meniju zaslona izbereš predvajalnik ali kazalec; izbira se zapomni za vsak program. Seja preživi kratek izpad povezave (do 30 s). Potreben je Safeer Control 2.1.1. **Tablica pokonci:** Nastavitve, Videz in aplikacije TV se zasukajo, mreža Aplikacij ima 4 stolpce.
