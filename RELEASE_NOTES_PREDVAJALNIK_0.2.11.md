# Safeer Predvajalnik 0.2.11

- **Sprotno pretvarjanje:** video, ki ga naprava ne zna predvajati, sproti pretvori naprava v Safeer Linku z najboljšim strojnim kodirnikom; Predvajalnik tako pomoč tudi nudi, ko je edina Safeer aplikacija na napravi.
- Medijski center: filmi in serije iz javnega kataloga Cinemeta ob dodatkih Stremio, odstranljivi vgrajeni PeerTube strežniki, imeniki dodatkov niso viri.

English: Live transcoding – a video the device cannot play is converted on the fly by the Safeer Link device with the best hardware encoder; the player also offers this help when it is the only Safeer app on the device. Media center: movies and series from the public Cinemeta catalogue with Stremio add-ons, removable built-in PeerTube servers, add-on directories are not sources.
