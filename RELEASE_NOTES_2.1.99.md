# Safeer Browser for Android TV 2.1.99

**Lighter on memory, steadier with other devices — and the first Safeer OS.**

- Tabs in the background go to sleep; after 10 minutes their page view is released and rebuilt when you return, so the TV keeps more free memory.
- Fixed: the picture could freeze after another device sent a page to a running Safeer (a delayed pause landed after the resume).
- The Safeer Link hub starts again by itself after an update of the browser.
- Hub: up to 16 devices at once (TV, tablet, phone, computer, Safeer Control, Safeer OS); before, the seventh was refused.
- Apps signed with the same key (Safeer OS) get their Safeer Link token from this browser without a code; the browser can open the Safeer Link page on request.
- **Safeer OS 0.1.0** (separate APK in this release, package `si.safeer.os`): a home screen for your TV built on Safeer Link — Web (Safeer Browser), Files from your computer (folders shared by Safeer Control: films, music and photos play straight from the computer, encrypted, home network only), Safeer Link, your devices and the TV's apps. Needs Safeer Browser for Android TV on the same TV. First preview.
