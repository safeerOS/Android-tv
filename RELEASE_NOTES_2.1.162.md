# Safeer OS 0.5.38 (TV, tablica, telefon) · Safeer Browser za Android TV 2.1.162

- **Splet: stranska vrstica ima spet ikone.** V Spletu je bila stranska vrstica prazna – ostala sta le znak Safeer in gumb za skrivanje, ikon Domov, Medijski center, Safeer Link, Sporočila, Programi, Datoteke, Splet, Zapiski in Nastavitve pa ni bilo videti (dotik na prazno mesto jih je sicer odprl). Ikone barvo berejo iz izbrane teme Safeer OS, Splet pa je tekel v temi brskalnika, ki teh barv nima. Zdaj je vrstica v Spletu enaka kot na drugih zaslonih in sledi izbrani temi.

Safeer Browser za Android TV 2.1.162 je brez sprememb v brskalniku (ista koda, nova številka). Safeer Predvajalnik ostane 0.2.29.

English: in Web, the side bar showed only the Safeer mark and the hide button – the section icons (Home, Media Center, Safeer Link, Messages, Apps, Files, Web, Notes, Settings) were invisible, although tapping the empty spots still opened them. The icons take their colour from the selected Safeer OS theme, which the browser screen did not have. The bar in Web now looks the same as on every other screen and follows the selected theme.
