# Safeer Browser for Android TV 2.1.115 · Safeer OS 0.3.3

**A clearer message when the computer is off, and a big clean-up under the hood.**

- **"The computer is not connected".** When you open a program or a file from the computer while the computer is not in Safeer Link, Safeer OS now says so plainly: turn the computer on and connect it to Safeer Link, or carry on with the TV.
- **The "How should Safeer OS work?" window no longer appears twice** if the screen turns off and on again while it is open.
- **Android Lint is clean.** All 455 old Lint errors and almost all warnings are fixed (the remaining four are library updates that need their own test round). Among the fixes: text is no longer glued together in code (symbols and numbers come from translatable resources), buttons in the phone menu have labels for screen readers, the address field does not offer autofill, unused images, layouts and texts are removed, screens draw their background once instead of twice, "..." is a real ellipsis everywhere, and the backup rules now state explicitly that no app data is backed up (as before: history and settings stay on the TV).
- Safeer OS Tablet stays at 0.2.2 in this release.

Slovensko: **»Računalnik ni povezan«.** Ko odpreš program ali datoteko z računalnika, računalnika pa ni v Safeer Linku, Safeer OS to pove naravnost: prižgi računalnik in ga poveži v Safeer Link ali nadaljuj na televizorju. **Okno »Kako naj Safeer OS deluje?« se ne pokaže več dvakrat**, če se zaslon vmes ugasne in prižge. **Android Lint je čist:** odpravljenih je vseh 455 starih napak in skoraj vsa opozorila (ostajajo štiri posodobitve knjižnic, ki potrebujejo svoj krog preizkusov). Med drugim: besedilo se v kodi ne lepi več skupaj (simboli in številke pridejo iz prevedljivih virov), gumbi v meniju na telefonu imajo opise za bralnike zaslona, polje za naslov ne ponuja samodejnega izpolnjevanja, odstranjene so neuporabljene slike, postavitve in besedila, zasloni rišejo ozadje enkrat namesto dvakrat, »...« je povsod pravo tropičje, pravila za varnostne kopije zdaj izrecno povedo, da se podatki aplikacije ne kopirajo (kot doslej: zgodovina in nastavitve ostanejo na televizorju). Safeer OS Tablet v tej izdaji ostane 0.2.2.
