# Safeer Browser for Android TV 2.1.102

**The Shield now answers on TCP too, keeps working when a resolver goes quiet, and says exactly what it does.**

- **DNS over TCP.** A resolver switches to TCP when the UDP answer does not fit in one packet. Until now the Shield dropped those packets and the lookup simply went nowhere — a page would fail to open for no visible reason. The Shield now handles that path itself: it answers the connection, takes the question, asks your network's resolver over TCP and returns the answer. A blocked domain is still blocked over TCP, so this is not a way around the filter.
- **If the first resolver goes quiet, the next one is asked.** Home routers stop answering DNS more often than you would think, usually after a connection drop. The Shield now repeats the question to the next resolver after 1.2 seconds instead of waiting for a timeout that never resolves.
- **The reply check is finished.** A reply is accepted only if it comes from the server that was asked, from port 53, carries our own transaction ID, has the response flag set, and repeats exactly our question — name, type and class. The matching logic now lives in one pure function with regression tests behind it: wrong server, wrong port, wrong ID, wrong name, wrong type, wrong class, a query posing as a reply, and two apps that happened to pick the same DNS ID.
- **The privacy policy describes the Shield.** What the local VpnService is and is not, where allowed queries go, and where the filter's limits are (an app with its own DNS-over-HTTPS, or one connecting straight to an IP address, does not pass through it).

Slovensko: **DNS prek TCP.** Razreševalec preide na TCP, kadar odgovor po UDP ne gre v en paket; doslej je Ščit take pakete zavrgel in poizvedba je padla v prazno — stran se ni odprla brez vidnega razloga. Zdaj Ščit to pot obvlada sam: sprejme povezavo, prevzame vprašanje, vpraša razreševalca tvojega omrežja po TCP in vrne odgovor. Blokirana domena ostane blokirana tudi po TCP, tako da to ni obhod filtra. **Če prvi razreševalec utihne, vpraša naslednjega** — domači usmerjevalniki radi nehajo odgovarjati po prekinitvi povezave; Ščit zdaj po 1,2 sekunde ponovi vprašanje pri naslednjem namesto da bi čakal v prazno. **Preverjanje odgovora je dokončano:** odgovor sprejmemo samo, če pride s strežnika, ki smo ga vprašali, z vrat 53, nosi naš ID, ima zastavico odgovora in ponovi natanko naše vprašanje (ime, vrsta in razred); ta logika je zdaj v eni čisti funkciji z regresijskimi testi (napačen strežnik, vrata, ID, ime, vrsta, razred, poizvedba namesto odgovora, dve aplikaciji z istim ID-jem). **Politika zasebnosti opisuje Ščit:** kaj krajevni VpnService je in kaj ni, kam gredo dovoljene poizvedbe in kje so meje filtriranja.
