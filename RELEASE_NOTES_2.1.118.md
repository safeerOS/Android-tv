# Safeer Browser for Android TV 2.1.118 · Safeer OS 0.4.1 · Safeer OS Tablet 0.3.1

**Any phone can join – no Safeer app needed – and every device can be named.**

- **Safeer Link Web.** Open *Devices → Connect a new device* on the TV, tablet or computer and point any phone camera at the code: the phone opens a small web page served by your own Safeer Link (no install, no cloud, home network only) with a remote control, "open this link on the TV" and text sharing. A phone with Safeer Browser opens the same code in the app.
- **Name your devices.** In *Devices* (TV and tablet), on the Safeer Link page and in the web client you can rename any device, including the one you are holding. The name is kept by Safeer Link, so every phone, computer and TV shows the same one.
- **Files from phone and tablet.** *Files* in Safeer OS now lists the videos, music and pictures of connected phones and tablets, not only computers – with a switch on each device to allow it.
- **Computer sign-in with a QR code, no cloud.** A computer joins Safeer Link by scanning the code shown by Safeer OS; the request is confirmed on the TV or tablet. Safeer OS also works without connecting anything – connect devices whenever you like.
- **Computer sound on the TV or tablet** (audio.play), with a shorter buffer (159 ms instead of 304 ms).
- **Safeer OS opens every time the TV wakes up**, apps of connected devices sit in their own row, an app can be streamed to the sender (*Stream app*), and the tablet accepts touch, swipes and keys from the computer (*Safeer Input*).
- **Safeer Link core:** every device has its own key (circle of trust – no new code after a hub change), the hub is elected automatically by priority, session tokens for signed logins, one shared source for the hub on TV and phone.
- **YouTube on TV:** thumbnails are visible again.

Slovensko: **Safeer Link Web** – na TV, tablici ali računalniku odpri *Naprave → Poveži novo napravo* in s kamero kateregakoli telefona poskeniraj kodo: telefon odpre majhno spletno stran, ki jo streže tvoj Safeer Link (brez namestitve, brez oblaka, samo domače omrežje) z daljincem, »odpri na TV« in pošiljanjem besedila; telefon s Safeer Browserjem isto kodo odpre v aplikaciji. **Poimenuj naprave:** v *Napravah* (TV in tablica), na strani Safeer Link in v spletnem odjemalcu preimenuješ katerokoli napravo, tudi to, ki jo držiš; ime hrani Safeer Link, zato ga vse naprave kažejo enako. **Datoteke s telefona in tablice:** videi, glasba in slike povezanih telefonov in tablic v Datotekah Safeer OS. **Prijava računalnika s QR kodo brez oblaka**, potrditev na TV ali tablici; Safeer OS deluje tudi brez povezanih naprav. **Zvok računalnika na TV/tablici** s krajšim medpomnilnikom. Safeer OS se odpre ob vsakem vklopu TV, programi naprav v svoji vrstici, *Pretoči aplikacijo*, *Safeer Vnos* na tablici. **Jedro Safeer Link:** ključ vsake naprave (krog zaupanja – po menjavi središča ni nove kode), samodejna izvolitev središča, sejni žetoni, en vir za hub na TV in telefonu. **YouTube na TV:** sličice spet vidne.
