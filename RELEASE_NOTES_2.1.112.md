# Safeer Browser for Android TV 2.1.112 · Safeer OS 0.2.12 · Safeer OS Tablet 0.2.0

**Safeer OS: computer programs get their own screen, the controller really works, and programs close from the TV.**

- **A separate screen for programs from the computer.** A program you start from Safeer OS opens on a separate, invisible screen on the computer (Safeer Control 2.0.7 with `sway` and `wf-recorder` installed), so whoever sits at the computer keeps their desktop. "Computer screen" still shows the whole desktop.
- **More than one program:** "Next program" in the session menu switches between them. When the last program closes (for example a game that quits on Escape), Safeer OS returns to its home screen and says so, instead of leaving a dark screen.
- **Close programs from the TV.** Programs in the Continue row carry a visible ✕, and a "Close all" button next to the row's title closes every program started from the TV on the computer.
- **The controller's D-pad jumps between buttons.** In a program on the TV screen (pointer mode, with Safeer Control 2.0.7) the D-pad moves to the next button or field in that direction and A clicks it — in the calculator you walk over the digits and operations. In a game (key mode) the D-pad stays the arrow keys, held as long as you hold it, and the sticks always move the mouse.
- **The controller works like keyboard and mouse.** Its D-pad is the arrow keys (held as long as you hold it), both sticks move the mouse (the right one slower, for precise aiming), R2 and L2 click, L1 and R1 scroll. For a game that wants a real gamepad, choose "Controller as a gamepad" in the session menu — Safeer OS remembers it for that program. When the on-screen keyboard is open, the D-pad and stick move around it like the remote. A new "Test the buttons" screen in Settings shows each connected controller and what every button and stick sends.
- **Games start with the arrows as arrows.** A game from the computer opens in key mode, so the remote's arrows move the piece or the player instead of the mouse; if you switch the mode for a program, Safeer OS remembers it for next time.
- **The remote jumps between buttons, like on Android.** In a program on the computer's TV screen (pointer mode, Safeer Control 2.0.7) a short press of an arrow jumps to the next button, link or field in that direction; holding it moves the pointer freely. A short press no longer throws the pointer past its target — on the full desktop it is now one small, precise step.
- **Ending the session closes the program on the computer.** Hold Back or choose "End" in the session menu, and the programs started from the TV close on the computer too. A program with unsaved work may still ask, and then stays in the Continue row.
- **The TV draws the selection.** When the remote or the controller jumps to a button in a computer program, Safeer OS draws a clear frame around it, exactly on the button (Safeer Control 2.0.7). The frame stays after OK — in the calculator you keep pressing the same key — and disappears as soon as the pointer moves. A new dialog starts on its default button, and at the bottom or top of a long page the page scrolls on instead of stopping.
- **The on-screen keyboard opens by itself** when you press OK on a text field you jumped to.
- **Zoom and drag** in the session menu: zoom 2× around the pointer, drawn on the TV, and drag with the pointer (OK drops).
- **Coloured keys in a browser on the computer:** red back, green forward, yellow reload, blue new tab (the hint appears only if your remote has these keys). Channel up and down are page up and down.
- **The Continue row shows ✕ only for programs that are really running**, and when a session ends you are back on the card you opened.
- **New devices can join from Safeer OS.** When a tablet, phone or computer wants to join Safeer Link on this TV, Safeer OS shows the six-digit code itself, over whatever is on screen; before, the code appeared only while the browser was open.
- Fixed: after Safeer Control restarted, reopening a program could show the computer's desktop instead; now the TV says the program is no longer open and goes home.
- **Safeer OS Tablet 0.2.0 (preview).** The tablet finds Safeer Link on your TV by itself and joins it with the code shown on the TV. The computer's screen is controlled by touch: tap to click, drag with a finger, hold for a right click, two fingers scroll; the ☰ button opens the session menu and the tablet's own keyboard types into the computer.
- Safeer Browser for Android TV: lets Safeer OS show the pairing code for a new device; no other changes.
- **The controller reaches every program in the list.** In "Computer programs" the controller's stick and D-pad now move from card to card and row to row exactly like the remote; before, they jumped out of the list to the categories and the second row could not be reached.
- **Start menu** is the first item in the session menu on the computer's desktop.
- **Search in "Apps on this TV"**, and moving between categories in computer programs no longer jumps when you come back up from the list.
- Transfer-speed numbers are no longer shown over the computer's picture, and the hints about the keys appear only the first time you open the computer's screen, not with every program.
- The home screen's top line stays visible when you move into the Continue row, and moving right past its last card no longer drops you into the row below.

Slovensko: **programi z računalnika dobijo svoj zaslon.** Program, ki ga zaženeš iz Safeer OS, se odpre na ločenem, nevidnem zaslonu računalnika (Safeer Control 2.0.7 z nameščenima `sway` in `wf-recorder`), zato kdor sedi za računalnikom, obdrži svoje namizje; »Zaslon računalnika« še vedno pokaže celo namizje. Med več programi preklopiš z »Naslednji program« v meniju seje, in ko se zadnji program zapre (npr. igra ob Esc), se Safeer OS vrne na domači zaslon namesto na temen zaslon. **Programe zapreš s televizorja:** v vrstici Nadaljuj imajo viden ✕, gumb »Zapri vse« ob naslovu vrste pa zapre vse, kar je bilo zagnano s televizorja. **Križec kontrolerja skače po gumbih:** v programu na zaslonu televizorja (način kazalca) križec skoči na naslednji gumb ali polje v tisti smeri, A ga klikne – v kalkulatorju se sprehajaš po številkah in operacijah; v igri (način tipk) so križec puščice, palici pa vedno premikata miško. **Kontroler deluje kot tipkovnica in miška:** smerni križec so puščice (držane, dokler držiš), obe palici premikata miško (desna počasneje, za natančen zadetek), R2 in L2 klikneta, L1 in R1 drsita; za igro, ki hoče pravi igralni plošček, v meniju seje izbereš »Plošček kot igralni plošček« in Safeer OS si to zapomni za ta program. Ko je odprta zaslonska tipkovnica, se križec in palica po njej premikata kot daljinec. Novi zaslon »Preizkusi tipke« v nastavitvah pokaže vsak povezan kontroler in kaj pošlje vsak gumb. **Igre se odprejo v načinu tipk** (puščice daljinca so puščice, ne miška), izbiro načina pa si Safeer OS zapomni za vsak program. **Daljinec skače po gumbih kot na Androidu:** v programu na zaslonu računalnika kratek pritisk puščice skoči na naslednji gumb, povezavo ali polje, držanje pa kazalec vodi prosto; kratek pritisk kazalca ne odnese več mimo cilja. **Konec seje zapre program tudi na računalniku** (drži Nazaj ali »Končaj«); program z neshranjenim delom lahko še vpraša in ostane v vrstici Nadaljuj. **Kontroler doseže vse programe na seznamu:** v »Programih računalnika« se palica in križec premikata od kartice do kartice in od vrste do vrste natanko kot daljinec; prej sta skočila iz seznama na kategorije in druga vrsta je bila nedosegljiva. **Meni Start** je prvi v meniju seje, **iskanje** v »Aplikacije na tem televizorju«, premik med kategorijami programov ne skače več, številk o hitrosti prenosa čez sliko ni več, namiga o tipkah pa se pokažeta samo prvič, ne ob vsakem programu. **Televizor nariše izbiro:** ko daljinec ali kontroler skoči na gumb v programu z računalnika, Safeer OS okoli njega nariše jasen okvir, natanko na gumbu (Safeer Control 2.0.7); po OK ostane, ob premiku kazalca izgine. Novo pogovorno okno začne na privzetem gumbu, na dnu ali vrhu dolge strani pa se stran premakne naprej. **Zaslonska tipkovnica se odpre sama**, ko pritisneš OK na polju za besedilo. **Povečava in vlečenje** v meniju seje. **Barvne tipke v brskalniku na računalniku:** rdeča nazaj, zelena naprej, rumena osveži, modra nov zavihek; program gor/dol sta stran gor/dol. **✕ v vrstici Nadaljuj samo pri programih, ki res tečejo**, po koncu seje pa je izbira spet na kartici. **Nova naprava se poveže iz Safeer OS:** kodo za seznanitev Safeer OS pokaže sam, čez karkoli je na zaslonu. Popravljeno: po ponovnem zagonu Safeer Controla je ponovno odprt program lahko pokazal namizje računalnika; zdaj televizor pove, da program ni več odprt. **Safeer OS Tablet 0.2.0 (predogled):** tablica sama najde Safeer Link na televizorju in se mu pridruži s kodo s televizorja; zaslon računalnika upravljaš z dotikom (dotik klikne, vlečenje s prstom vleče, držanje je desni klik, dva prsta drsita), gumb ☰ odpre meni seje, tipkovnica tablice piše v računalnik. Brskalnik za Android TV le omogoči, da Safeer OS pokaže kodo za novo napravo.
