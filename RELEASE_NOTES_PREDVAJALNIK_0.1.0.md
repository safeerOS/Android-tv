# Safeer Predvajalnik 0.1.0 (Android)

**The Safeer media player as an app of its own** for phones and tablets: the same Safeer Media as inside Safeer OS, without the launcher.

- Opens straight into Safeer Media: music from independent artists, radio, PeerTube videos, podcasts, your own sources and files.
- "Open with": video, audio, HLS and DASH from other apps (files and web addresses) play in Safeer Predvajalnik; music keeps playing in the background.
- Add-ons (Stremio, Kodi): two clearly labelled fields in *My sources* where you enter the addresses of **your own** add-ons. Safeer ships no catalogue; the addresses stay on your device.
- Playback engine: Android Media3 (ExoPlayer) with hardware decoding where the device offers it.
- Installs alongside Safeer OS (separate package `si.safeer.player`); no account, no cloud, no telemetry.

Slovensko: **Safeer Predvajalnik kot samostojna aplikacija** za telefon in tablico: isti Safeer Media kot v Safeer OS, brez zaganjalnika. Odpre se naravnost v Safeer Media (glasba neodvisnih izvajalcev, radio, PeerTube, podkasti, tvoji viri in datoteke); **»Odpri z«** za video, zvok, HLS in DASH iz drugih aplikacij, glasba igra naprej v ozadju. **Dodatki (Stremio, Kodi):** dve jasno označeni polji v *Mojih virih*, kamor vneseš naslove svojih dodatkov – Safeer ne prilaga nobenega kataloga, naslovi ostanejo na napravi. Pogon Media3 (ExoPlayer) s strojnim dekodiranjem, kjer ga naprava ponuja. Namesti se poleg Safeer OS (ločen paket `si.safeer.player`); brez računa, oblaka in telemetrije.

Preverjeno na telefonu z Androidom 14 (namestitev, »Odpri z«, dodatki). Na televizorju ta aplikacija ni namenjena uporabi – tam je Safeer OS.
