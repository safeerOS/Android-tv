# Safeer Predvajalnik 0.2.13

- Safeer Link: en krog zaupanja (po ponovni seznanitvi naprava po ponovnem zagonu ne ostane brez kroga), mreža brez trkanja na stare identitete; glave Stremio (`proxyHeaders`) tudi na segmentih HLS/DASH na drugem gostitelju.

English: Safeer Link keeps a single trust circle (a re-paired device no longer loses it after restart), mesh calls back off instead of knocking on stale identities; Stremio `proxyHeaders` also reach HLS/DASH segments on other hosts.
