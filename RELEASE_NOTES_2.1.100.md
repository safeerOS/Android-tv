# Safeer Browser for Android TV 2.1.100

**Safeer Shield for the whole TV, and Safeer OS built in — one install, no second app.**

- **Safeer Shield**: ad, tracker and botnet filtering for the **whole television**, not just the browser. It is a local DNS filter (~436,000 domains from hagezi PRO, Threat Intelligence and Fake lists, URLhaus, Phishing Army and SI-CERT): blocked names simply do not resolve, in every app on the TV. Nothing leaves the television — no traffic is routed anywhere, the filter answers on the device itself. Turn it on from the browser menu or from the Safeer OS home screen; it starts with the TV.
- **Safeer OS is now part of this app.** No separate APK any more: one install puts two entries in your TV launcher — Safeer Browser and Safeer OS. If you have the old standalone Safeer OS (package `si.safeer.os`) installed, you can uninstall it.
- **It works with or without Safeer Link.** If Safeer Link is already running in the browser, Safeer OS simply joins it — no code, no second connection. If it is not running, Safeer OS asks once: turn Safeer Link on, or work **locally** with this television alone.
- **Files on the TV itself**: in local mode, Safeer OS shows the videos, music and photos on the television and on a plugged-in USB stick, and plays them straight away. With Safeer Link you still get the folders shared by Safeer Control on your computer.
- **Web apps**: add a page and it behaves like an app — its own icon and name from the site's web app manifest, full screen without the browser bar, and the same protection as the browser (ad and tracker blocking, dangerous sites, Safeer Shield). Add one from the browser menu ("Add to Safeer OS apps") or from the Safeer OS home screen. A web app continues where you left off for half an hour.
- Web apps can also appear as a row of cards on the television's own home screen, on TVs that support app channels there.

Slovensko: **Safeer Ščit** filtrira oglase, sledilce in botnete za **ves televizor**, ne le za brskalnik — krajevni filter DNS z okoli 436.000 domenami (hagezi PRO, Threat Intelligence in Fake, URLhaus, Phishing Army, SI-CERT); blokirana imena se preprosto ne razrešijo, v vseh aplikacijah televizorja. Nič ne zapusti televizorja: promet se ne preusmerja nikamor, filter odgovarja na napravi sami. Vklopiš ga v meniju brskalnika ali na domačem zaslonu Safeer OS; zažene se skupaj s televizorjem. **Safeer OS je zdaj del te aplikacije** — ni več ločenega APK-ja: ena namestitev doda dva vnosa v zaganjalnik (Safeer Browser in Safeer OS). Če imaš nameščen stari samostojni Safeer OS (paket `si.safeer.os`), ga lahko odstraniš. **Deluje s Safeer Linkom ali brez njega**: če Safeer Link že teče v brskalniku, se Safeer OS vanj pridruži sam, brez kode in brez nove povezave; če ne teče, enkrat vpraša — vklopi Safeer Link ali delaj **krajevno**, samo s tem televizorjem. **Datoteke televizorja**: v krajevnem načinu Safeer OS pokaže videe, glasbo in slike na televizorju in na priklopljenem USB ključu ter jih predvaja; s Safeer Linkom so tu še mape, ki jih deli Safeer Control na računalniku. **Spletne aplikacije**: dodana stran se obnaša kot aplikacija — svoja ikona in ime iz manifesta spletne aplikacije, čez ves zaslon brez vrstice brskalnika, z isto zaščito kot brskalnik; dodaš jo v meniju brskalnika (»Dodaj med aplikacije Safeer OS«) ali na domačem zaslonu Safeer OS, in pol ure nadaljuje tam, kjer si končal. Na televizorjih, ki to podpirajo, se spletne aplikacije pokažejo tudi kot vrsta kartic na domačem zaslonu televizorja.
