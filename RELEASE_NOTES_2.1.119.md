# Safeer Browser for Android TV 2.1.119 · Safeer OS 0.4.2 · Safeer OS Tablet 0.3.2

**Fix: a device that reconnects after the hub restarts is never left invisible.**

- **Safeer Link hub:** when a device comes back with a new connection (after the hub or the device restarted), the new connection replaces the old one and the old one is closed. Before, a device could stay connected yet disappear from *Devices* on the TV and its programs could not be opened (seen with a computer running Safeer Control 2.0.8 or older after a TV app update).
- Everything from 2.1.118 (Safeer Link Web, device names, files from phone and tablet) is unchanged.

Slovensko: **Popravek:** naprava, ki se po ponovnem zagonu središča poveže znova, ne more več ostati nevidna – nova povezava zamenja staro, stara se zapre. Prej je računalnik lahko ostal povezan, a ga v *Napravah* na TV ni bilo in njegovih programov ni bilo mogoče odpreti (opaženo s Safeer Controlom 2.0.8 ali starejšim po posodobitvi aplikacije na TV). Vse iz 2.1.118 ostaja.
